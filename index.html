About this app

Odialer is RecreateTM’s independent recreation of the ColorOS 17 dialer experience, focused on a refined interface and a smooth everyday calling workflow.

Communication
Odialer is an independent project by RecreateTM and is not an official OPPO or ColorOS application. Availability of calling features depends on your Android version, device, permissions and the app build.
  • Download the Android APK from this page.
  • Review Android's installation prompt before installing.
  • Join the Telegram community for project updates and help.
